title: Oil Field Investment Optimization
description: |
  Developed a machine learning model to optimize a $100M oil well investment decision across three regions.
  Used Linear Regression to predict reserve volumes and applied bootstrap simulation (1000 iterations) to evaluate financial risk under uncertainty.

  Results:
  Region 1 selected based on highest expected profit (~$4.61M), 
  lowest RMSE (~0.89), 
  and lowest probability of loss (~0.23%) under a 95% confidence interval framework.

  This project demonstrates risk-adjusted capital allocation using predictive modeling and uncertainty simulation.
